# 📺 Alice in Borderland

![Alice in Borderland](https://via.placeholder.com/600x300/8B0000/ffffff?text=Alice+in+Borderland)

## **Overview**

*Alice in Borderland* is a **Japanese sci-fi thriller** series based on the manga by **Haro Aso**. The show follows Ryōhei Arisu and his friends in a parallel Tokyo where they must compete in deadly games to survive.

---

## **Plot Summary**

> "Welcome to the Borderland. Every game is a matter of life and death."

**Ryōhei Arisu** is transported to an empty Tokyo where survival depends on winning deadly games. Each game is categorized by playing card suits:

- ♠️ **Spades** - Physical games
- ♥️ **Hearts** - Psychological games  
- ♣️ **Clubs** - Team games
- ♦️ **Diamonds** - Intelligence games

## **Main Characters**

#### **Ryōhei Arisu** (Kento Yamazaki)
- Protagonist and skilled strategist
- Specializes in ♦️ Diamond games

#### **Yuzuha Usagi** (Tao Tsuchiya)  
- Skilled climber and survivor
- Arisu's closest ally

#### **Shuntarō Chishiya** (Nijiro Murakami)
- Highly intelligent medical student
- Calculative thinker
